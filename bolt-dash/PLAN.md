## Bolt TV (in-game movie platform, later)
- Original series first. "My movies" = the traditional movie of each player's tournament (10 minutes, built automatically from recorded matches and life events). Later genres: horror, action, comedy, sports. More GPUs, voices and paid tools once the game grows.
- **Horror villain — the Mad Prisoner:** same base character recoloured: black/red scary colours, red face, frightening eyes, black blood (dark-character rule). Always wears a hockey-style mask with eye holes and many small holes; in his worst madness he wears a carved pumpkin mask. Mostly disguised by day, rarely at night. Walks at night attacking anyone he sees; jailed 4 times, escaped every time leaving dead or maimed policemen. Weapons: kitchen cleaver and a chainsaw. (Models to find on GitHub: hockey mask, chainsaw; found: carved pumpkin, cleaver, knives, axe.)

## Slasher Night (special long mode, planned)
- Listed with the mini-games but marked special. One long map, no time limit: it ends when someone beats the boss or everyone is dead.
- Play with bots (friends online later). Weapon: your bat only. Die once and you are out of the run.
- 5 stages at night, each harder: forest (zombies, skeletons) · abandoned city streets (small Mad Prisoner copies with cleavers chasing you) · abandoned hospital/house (ghosts jumping out) · the prison yard he escaped from (everything at once, more and faster copies) · final fight.
- Boss: the Mad Prisoner. Phase 1 hockey mask + cleaver; at half health he goes mad: pumpkin + chainsaw, faster. Health bar must reach zero.
- Reward: the rare Mad Prisoner skin as an achievement, only from winning this mode, and only for players still alive at the end (not shop, wheel or codes).
- The chainsaw is always running (animated); touching it = instant death.
- Boss attacks (many): cleaver swing, cleaver throw (comes back), jump slam shockwave you must jump over, charge dash, grab-and-throw, whistle that calls copies; mad phase adds chainsaw spin, chainsaw lunge, ground-cut lines, pumpkin bombs, rage roar that slows everyone.
- Arena turns into mini-games mid-fight (every ~30 s, warning first): tiles crack and fall (Hex Drop) · lava tiles glowing red then dropping · cannons from the walls · spinning arms and saws · arena edges falling (shrinking) · lights out with only the boss's red eyes visible · conveyor/ice floor.
- Colours: varied but dark (deep purple, blood red, dark teal, burnt orange, swamp green, midnight blue) with glowing edges so it stays readable.
- Look ready: hockey mask ("Jason Mask", ShadowSupreme), carved pumpkin, cleaver, chainsaw ✅ (film/epx.js).

## Life game (linked, not merged)
- Two games linked through one project (same save: coins, outfits, player card); 200 coins gift the first time you open the other game.
- Explore a city, live, shops (classic clothes, national kits, sports), food and drink that affect your next matches a little (fast food hurts if overdone; a fictional "dizzy drink" reverses controls and gives silly jumps).
- During tournaments your day starts in the life game: wake up, morning TV with the table, drive to the stadium (short and skippable after day one).
- Jobs (delivery, shop, taxi, coach, commentator, bodyguard, police/detective, pro player). Travel between the nations (plane/ship; cheap is riskier, nothing is 100% safe; accidents become survival scenes). Each nation is famous for one game and has its venue.
- Permadeath: if you are killed you start over with a new character, but you keep a hall-of-fame entry and get a "revenge file" to investigate and hunt your killer. Hitmen are very expensive, police, secret police and cameras everywhere, safe places (home, stadium, hospital), bodyguards, warnings. Night streets riskier (by fictional nation).
- Business: famous rich players buy land and build venues brick by brick (materials, motors, workers) and earn from visitors; host tournaments, sponsor teams. Fame ranking; the most famous player gets a personal skin and a statue.
- Bolt Dash build mode stays free building; player maps are only promoted when players love them (record likes) ✅ (likes + promotion exist).
- Online multiplayer needed for player-vs-player features; build with bots first.

## Open world (agreed plan, all in one batch)
- Separate model files that load only when entering the world (game start stays light).
- **Entering the world:** from the menu a swirling vortex opens behind you, made of pieces of the menu; a beam of light pulls you in. From inside a game the same vortex shows pieces of that game's picture as you're pulled out.
- City island: downtown, shops, stadium (doors = mini-games), park, beach, port; day/night; NPCs; drivable cars.
- **Food (many, each with a small effect for the next match):** shawarma, burger, koshari, foul & taameya, pizza, feteer, hawawshi, liver sandwich, sushi, ice cream, kunafa, basbousa.
- **Drinks (generic names, no real brands):** cola, orange juice, mango, sobia, tamarind, licorice, tea, coffee, energy drink, plus the fictional dizzy drink (reverses controls for a while).
- Buy homes: flat → house → villa; home = spawn point, mirror wardrobe, garage.
- Jobs: delivery first, then taxi, shop worker, coach, commentator, bodyguard, police/detective, pro player.
- Phase 2 in the same batch: police and cameras, permadeath + revenge file, travel risk, business (build venues), fame ranking + statue.
- Skins in the same batch: Cosmos (moving space on the body, 25,000 coins, Rare); owner-only Invisible (only worn clothes show; pet hides too). Half-ghost (eyes + small feathers) later.
- 100 foods + 50 drinks (Kenney Food Kit and others; some drinks share a bottle with a different colour/label).
- Enterable buildings: shops, restaurants, homes for sale, stadium, police station (others are decoration). Inside = a separate room scene built from furniture models.
- Some NPC homes are left unlocked (they forgot the key); you can walk in.
- Thief job: break into some homes (unlocked doors, climbing walls/windows); crimes raise your wanted level: alarms, neighbours calling, cameras, police chase.
- Homes come furnished or empty: empty ones need furniture bought from a furniture shop (sofa, TV, bed, kitchen, decor) and placed with a grid like build mode.
